Our concept

Puzzles designed to live
on your walls,
not in a box.

Brikke Studio was born from a simple idea: what if a puzzle could be a piece of art? We use AI to generate original, one-of-a-kind designs — bold, colorful, full of texture — that transform the Nordic-white walls of modern homes into living galleries.

Each puzzle is produced on demand and shipped worldwide. Build it, frame it, hang it. No two pieces are ever alike.

🎨
AI-generated, original artwork Every design is unique — created exclusively for Brikke Studio.
🖼️
Made to be framed Standard sizes that fit IKEA RIBBA and SANNAHED frames perfectly.
🌿
Print on demand Produced only when ordered — no excess inventory, less waste.
